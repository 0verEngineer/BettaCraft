# Reef invertebrates

The clean-up crew of a reef tank: the cleaner shrimp picks parasites off the fish, hermit crabs and turbo snails graze
algae and eat leftovers. They are [small animals](../invertebrates.md) like the freshwater ones (fish bag, the small
animals page of the menu), but live only in salt water and do not breed in a tank.

## Species

| Animal | °C | Breeding | Algae |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Pacific Cleaner Shrimp](cleaner_shrimp.md) | 23–28 | no | hardly |
| [Blue-Leg Hermit Crab](hermit_crab.md) | 22–28 | no | some |
| [Mexican Turbo Snail](turbo_snail.md) | 22–28 | no | a lot |
| [Fire Shrimp](fire_shrimp.md) | 24–28 | no | hardly |
