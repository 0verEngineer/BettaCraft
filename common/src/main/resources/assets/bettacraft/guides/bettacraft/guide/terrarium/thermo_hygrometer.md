# Thermo-hygrometer

<GameScene zoom="4" interactive={true}>
  <Block id="bettacraft:thermo_hygrometer" p:facing="north" />
</GameScene>

The <ItemLink id="bettacraft:thermo_hygrometer" /> is a small digital display. Stick it to a **wall of the tank** from the
outside. Its screen shows the **air temperature** and the **humidity** of the [terrarium or paludarium](../terrarium.md)
behind it (on an aquarium the water temperature).

* **Right-click** it: the kind of tank, air, humidity, basking spot and UV in one line.
* A **comparator** reads the humidity (0 to 15 for 0 to 100 %), for example to switch a [mister](mister.md) or a lamp with
  redstone.
