The <ItemLink id="bettacraft:led_panel" /> is the **middle light tier**, stronger than the [light bar](light.md).

* One item lays a panel **two blocks wide**: the second half lies away from you. Panels placed in a row along their
  length join into **one long panel**.
* It gives light 15 **plus 2.5** on the part of the aquarium it covers: red plants turn **redder**, the strong-light
  plants (Monte Carlo, Alternanthera) grow faster, and without CO2 and fertiliser the **algae** grow faster too.
* Dye it like the light bar (right-click with a dye colours the whole panel); redstone switches it off; dimmer and
  daylight timer in the tank menu.
* Directly on an aquarium it **covers** the blocks under it like a [lid](tank_lid.md): no evaporation there, nothing flies
  out or climbs in.
