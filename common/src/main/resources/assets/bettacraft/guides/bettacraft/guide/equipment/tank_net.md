# Aquarium net

<ItemImage id="bettacraft:tank_net" scale="2" />

The net works in two places:

* **In open water** (rivers, swamps, jungles, mangroves, beaches, caves, warm and lukewarm oceans) it catches small
  animals and fish into an **empty fish bag** from your inventory; see [Catching animals](../getting_started/catching.md)
  and [Where to find what](../getting_started/sources.md). Now and then it brings up an **aquarium plant** from the
  bottom of rivers, jungle water and swamps (Vallisneria, Monte Carlo, the Amazon sword, Alternanthera, the tiger lotus,
  crypts, Java moss; no bag needed) and on a reef a piece of coral.
* **On an aquarium** it takes out floating plants where you look, from any angle (sneaking thins the whole surface out
  by half), and aimed at a shrimp, crayfish, snail, clutch or shed shell it takes that out.

It wears a little with every swing in open water.
