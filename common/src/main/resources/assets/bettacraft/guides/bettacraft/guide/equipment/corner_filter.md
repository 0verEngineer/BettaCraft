# Corner filter

<ItemImage id="bettacraft:corner_filter" scale="2" />

The <ItemLink id="bettacraft:corner_filter" /> is a slim **motor filter** for the [interior](../interior.md)
(2x2x7 blocks at scale 16), best placed in a corner. Its intake slots are at the bottom, the outlet at the top faces
you when you place it.

* It keeps **20 water blocks** clean and moves the surface (a little oxygen).
* Like every motor filter it **sucks in young animals** unless an [intake sponge](intake_sponge.md) is fitted.
* **Power regulator:** right-click it or sneak + mouse wheel.
