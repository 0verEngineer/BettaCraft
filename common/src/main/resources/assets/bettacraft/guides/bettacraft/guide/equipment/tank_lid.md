# Tank lid

<ItemImage id="bettacraft:tank_lid" scale="2" />

A thin glass plate for the top of an aquarium. A top block with a lid (or the feeder, a light or any full block on it)
does not **evaporate**: the water level stays and you top up less often. The Water tab of the
[tank menu](../aquarium/menu.md) shows how many top blocks are still open. See [Water quality](../aquarium/water_quality.md).

The [light bar](light.md) can lie directly on a lid (put it onto the lid's top): the light needs no block of its own above
the lid.
