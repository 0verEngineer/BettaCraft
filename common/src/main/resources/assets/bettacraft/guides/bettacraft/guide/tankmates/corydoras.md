# Corydoras

Armoured catfish from South America that search the bottom in groups and now and then dash to the surface for air. After a cool water change the female glues her eggs onto the glass. The bronze cory is the hardiest; the panda, sterbai and bronze cory have bred forms (albino, longfin, gold laser).
