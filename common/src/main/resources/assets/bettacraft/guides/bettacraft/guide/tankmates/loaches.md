# Loaches

Bottom fish from South and South-East Asia: the eel-like kuhli loach is active at night and digs in the substrate, the big clown loach, the small dwarf chain loach and the reticulated loach live in groups and eat snails, and the garra and the small banded panda garra graze algae with their sucker mouths.
