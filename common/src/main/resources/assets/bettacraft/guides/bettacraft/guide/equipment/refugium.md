# Refugium

The **refugium** is a chamber of the [sump](sump.md) with **Chaetomorpha** (spaghetti algae) and a light of its own: a
[tank light](light.md), an [LED panel](led_panel.md) or a [high-power LED](high_power_led.md) in its light slot.

* **Nitrate:** every Chaetomorpha takes up **0.6 mg/L a day** in one block of water (`sump.refugiumNitratePerAlgae`), spread
  over the water of the tank and its sumps. A full chamber (16) in a tank of 8 blocks takes more than a normally stocked
  tank makes.
* **Algae on the glass:** the Chaetomorpha takes their nutrients (the phosphate): up to half less growth of the
  [glass algae](../aquarium/algae.md).
* **Growth:** lit and with the return pump running it doubles in about 4 days (`sump.chaetoDoublingDays`), slower towards a
  full chamber of 16.
* **Harvest:** take the extra ones out: **food** for shrimp and snails (right-click the tank), a vegetable for the
  [food maker](../food/food_maker.md), or into the **composter** (fertilizer).
* Without light (or with the pump standing still) it neither grows nor takes up anything. Switch: `features.sump.refugium`.
