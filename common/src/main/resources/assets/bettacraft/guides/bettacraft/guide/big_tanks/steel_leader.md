# Steel leader

<ItemImage id="bettacraft:steel_leader" scale="2" />

A short stainless steel wire with a swivel. Hold it in the **other hand** while you fish with any rod (also of other
mods): now and then (5 % of the catches, `bigAnimalRules.steelLeaderChance`) a **big fish** of that water bites instead of
the normal catch and goes into an empty [transport tub](transport_tub.md) in your inventory. Without an empty tub you
catch as usual. It wears a little with every big catch.
