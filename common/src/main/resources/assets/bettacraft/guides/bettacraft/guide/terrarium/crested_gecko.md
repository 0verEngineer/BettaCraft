# Crested gecko

The crested gecko (*Correlophus ciliatus*, about 20 cm) from the rainforest of New Caledonia was thought extinct until
1994. Spiny crests run over its eyes and down its back; its wide toe pads hold on smooth surfaces, so it **climbs the
glass** and the decoration.

## Care

| | |
|---|---|
| Tank | a [terrarium](../terrarium.md) (90 % land or more); height and decoration to climb on |
| Air | **19 to 27 °C** (it does not like heat: above 27 °C harms it); humidity 55 to 95 % |
| Food | soft fruit (sweet berries, glow berries, melon; `#bettacraft:gecko_fruit`), [crickets and fruit flies](feeder_insects.md) |
| Stocking | 2 bettas |

* It **climbs up the glass walls** and clings there, head up, for a while, then comes down again; it climbs straight up
  the faces of stones and wood and likes the high places of the land.
* By day it hides among grown [terrarium plants](plants.md) and under the decoration (`features.terrariumPlants.hiding`).

## Breeding

A pair lays two eggs into sand or soil; the sex is genetic. Morphs: **flame** (a cream back) and **harlequin** (cream
sides and legs) appear now and then; wild geckos are the brown tiger form.

## Obtaining

* <ItemLink id="bettacraft:crested_gecko_box" />: **Wild** on the floor of jungles, alone (spawn weight 3) (config `features.terrariums.wildHerps`).
