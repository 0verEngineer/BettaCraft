# First steps

## 1. Find a swamp

Wild <ItemLink id="bettacraft:betta_bucket" /> bettas live in swamps (also mangrove swamps and the swamp biomes of
other mods). They are small olive or bronze fish with short fins, the wild type "Plakat".

## 2. Catch a betta

Right-click a betta with a **water bucket** or a <ItemLink id="bettacraft:fish_bag" />. The bucket or bag keeps the fish
with all its genes. Fish in buckets and bags do not age.

<RecipesFor id="bettacraft:fish_bag" />

## 3. Build an aquarium

Craft <ItemLink id="bettacraft:tank" /> blocks from six glass of one kind and three iron ingots. Place several of
them next to each other: tanks made of the same glass join into one large aquarium.

<RecipeFor id="bettacraft:tank" />

## 4. Fill it with water

Right-click the aquarium with water buckets. Each bucket adds 1000 mB, the water rises from the bottom.
Animals may move in once the aquarium is at least half full.

## 5. Put the betta in

Right-click the aquarium with the betta bucket or the filled fish bag. Every adult needs about one water block;
from two adults per water block they get stressed.

## 6. Keep it warm

Bettas like 24 to 30 °C. Outside of warm biomes you need a [heater](equipment/heaters.md), placed in the
[interior](interior.md) or in an [external filter](equipment/filters.md). The tank menu (right-click with an empty hand)
shows the water temperature and lets you set the target temperature.

## 7. Feed it

Bettas need food at least every day. Right-click the aquarium with <ItemLink id="bettacraft:betta_pellets" /> or
<ItemLink id="bettacraft:mosquito_larvae" />, or let an <ItemLink id="bettacraft:feeder" /> do it.
See [Food](food.md) for how to get them.

That is all a single betta needs. Next: [decorate the interior](interior.md) or [start breeding](bettas/breeding.md).
