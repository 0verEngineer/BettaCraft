# Protein skimmer

<ItemImage id="bettacraft:protein_skimmer" scale="2" />

Hang it on a side of the tank like the [external filter](../equipment/filters.md). In salt water it foams the waste out:
an extra filter for 48 water blocks (config `saltwater.skimmerBlocks`). In fresh water nothing foams and it does nothing.
With power costs (`power.costs` `EASY` or `HARD`) it needs the power of a filter.
