# Corn snake

The corn snake (*Pantherophis guttatus*, 1.2 m) of the fields and pine woods of the south-east of North America is
harmless and calm: orange with red, black-bordered saddles and a black and white checkered belly. In the tank it is
shorter than in life.

## Care

| | |
|---|---|
| Tank | a dry [terrarium](../terrarium.md) (90 % land or more) of **8 blocks** or more for a grown one |
| Air | 21 to 31 °C with a [basking lamp](basking_lamp.md) as warm spot; humidity 35 to 70 % |
| Food | **a prey animal about once a week**: raw rabbit or raw chicken (`#bettacraft:snake_prey`, for the frozen mice of the hobby). A fed snake refuses food for five days |
| Stocking | a hatchling 0.4 bettas, growing to 4 |

* It slithers over the land in S-curves and rests long.
* **Brumation**: like Hermann's tortoise it rests through the cold (air below 12 °C, `terrarium.brumationBelow`): no food,
  no harm (only frost).

## Breeding

A pair lays 4-8 eggs into sand or soil; the sex is genetic. Morphs: **amelanistic** (no black, red eyes) and
**anerythristic** (no red: grey and black) appear now and then; wild snakes are the normal orange ones.

## Obtaining

* <ItemLink id="bettacraft:corn_snake_box" />: **Wild** in forests and plains, alone (spawn weight 3) (config `features.terrariums.wildHerps`).
