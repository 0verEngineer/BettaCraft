# Intake sponge

<ItemImage id="bettacraft:intake_sponge" scale="2" />

**Motor filters suck in young animals.** In an aquarium with young bettas, guppies or other fry, young shrimp or tadpoles of
the dwarf clawed frog, every running filter with a motor (internal filter, [corner filter](corner_filter.md), external
filter, filter in a cabinet) now and then sucks in one of them (logged in the tank log; snails and grown animals never).
The more power, the more often.

The <ItemLink id="bettacraft:intake_sponge" /> stops that: **use it on the filter** (on any block of an internal or
corner filter in the interior, on the external filter, or on a cabinet with a filter). It covers the intake; the filter
cleans as before. The internal filter shows it at its foot. It comes back when the filter is broken.

The [sponge filter](sponge_filter.md) never sucks anything in. The tab **Devices** of the tank menu lists the motor
filters by kind (group **Filter**) and how many of them have a sponge; hover the warning there to see how to fit one (item in
hand, right-click the filter; for a filter in a cabinet right-click the cabinet). The tab **Breeding** warns the same
while there are eggs or young animals. A server can switch the rule off (`features.breeding.fryIntake`) or change it
(`internalFilters.fryIntakePerDay`).
