# Large net

<ItemImage id="bettacraft:large_net" scale="2" />

The net for **big animals** (giant catfish, sharks, rays, dolphins). It needs an empty
[transport tub](transport_tub.md) in your inventory: the catch goes into it.

* **On a wild big animal:** right-click it to catch it.
* **In open water:** swung like the aquarium net it now and then brings up a big animal of that water (4 % per swing by
  default, `bigAnimalRules.largeNetChance`); see the Obtaining section of each big animal.
* Small fish go into the [aquarium net](../equipment/tank_net.md) and a fish bag.
