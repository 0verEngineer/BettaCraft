# Reeves' turtle

The Chinese three-keeled pond turtle (*Mauremys reevesii*, about 15 cm) lives in the ponds, rice paddies and slow rivers
of East Asia: a dark brown carapace with **three low keels** along the back, a yellow plastron with brown blotches and
yellow-green wavy lines on its head. It likes it a little cooler than the slider.

## Care

| | |
|---|---|
| Tank | a [paludarium](../terrarium.md): 20 to 60 % land; 6 blocks or more for a grown one |
| Water | 18 to 28 °C |
| Air | 18 to 30 °C |
| UV, basking spot | needs a [basking lamp](basking_lamp.md) (UV 2 or more; sun through an open top is enough) |
| Food | pellets, frozen and live food; [greens](hermann_tortoise.md#greens) |
| Stocking | a hatchling 0.4 bettas, growing to 4 |

* It goes into the tank from its bucket of water; a water bucket on a Reeves' turtle outside a tank catches it.
* It swims and walks on the bottom, climbs onto the land and basks.
* It grows up in 6 game days and lays eggs into sand or soil (see [turtle breeding](turtle_breeding.md)). Pivotal
  temperature 28.5 °C.

## Obtaining

* <ItemLink id="bettacraft:reeves_turtle_bucket" />: **Wild** in rivers, in groups of 1–2 (spawn weight 3) (config `features.turtles.wildSpawns`).
