# Magnifier

<ItemImage id="bettacraft:magnifier" scale="2" />

Every animal shows its look (variety, colours, fins), but the alleles behind it, the colours it carries hidden, stay
unknown until it is **analysed**: put the new animal into an [aquarium](../aquarium/inhabitants.md) and look at it
with the magnifier.

* **Right-click a tank** with the magnifier: every animal in it that is not analysed yet is analysed at once.
* **Tank menu**: select a fish (or a shrimp, crayfish or snail in the [list of small animals](../aquarium/menu.md))
  and press **Analyse**; the magnifier must be in your inventory.
* Every analysed animal uses the magnifier once: 64 uses, repaired at the anvil with glass panes.

The result stays with the animal for good: in the tank list, after moving it into another tank, and in the tooltips of
buckets and bags, which list every animal with its genes. Until then they show "genes not analysed". The breeding
prediction of a betta pair needs both parents analysed.

Animals of worlds from before the magnifier count as analysed, nobody loses what they could see. Server config
`wildlife.genesAlwaysVisible` shows every animal's genes without a magnifier; `features.breeding.magnifier` switches the whole
feature off.
