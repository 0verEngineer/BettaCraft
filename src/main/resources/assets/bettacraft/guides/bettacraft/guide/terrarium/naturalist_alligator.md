# Alligator (Naturalist)

The **alligator** of the Naturalist mod lives in a **big, warm paludarium**: water to swim in and land with a basking spot.
It needs only the mod and the shipped profile (`bettacraft:naturalist_alligator`).

| | |
|---|---|
| Tank | a [paludarium](../terrarium.md) (20 to 70 % land); a big animal of 2 m: at least **24 × 12 blocks, 6 high** and **800 blocks of tank** each (water and land together), a [big tank](../big_tanks.md) |
| Climate | air 24 to 36 °C, water 22 to 32 °C, humidity 50 % or more |
| Basking spot | a [basking lamp](basking_lamp.md) over the land (UV 2 or more) |
| Ground | sand or soil on the land (for its eggs) |
| Food | **raw meat and raw fish** (`#c:foods/raw_meat`, `#c:foods/raw_fish`), every three days |

* It is too big for the terrarium box: catch it with the [large net](../big_tanks/large_net.md) into a
  [transport tub](../big_tanks/transport_tub.md) and right-click the paludarium with the full tub.
* Feed it by hand on the tank or from a [feeder](../equipment/feeder.md) (the feeder takes raw meat).
* **A predator:** it eats the small fish of its tank (tankmates up to 0.8 blocks, bettas, dwarf frogs, small fish of other
  mods) and the shrimp, like the big predators of the [big tanks](../big_tanks.md); plants hide some. A meal feeds it.
* In the tank our walker moves it: it swims in the water, climbs onto the land and lies on the basking spot.

## Breeding

A fed, healthy pair breeds now and then. The female lays **2 to 4 eggs** into **sand or soil** on the land; at a nest of
32 °C they hatch in about 5 days (warmer faster; at 18 °C and below they wait, above 36 °C they die). Real alligators
have temperature-dependent sexes; the alligators of Naturalist have no sexes, so the nest temperature only sets the speed.
Their own breeding is suppressed in the tank.
