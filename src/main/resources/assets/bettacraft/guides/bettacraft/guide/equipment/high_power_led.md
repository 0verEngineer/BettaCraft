The <ItemLink id="bettacraft:high_power_led" /> is the **strongest light tier**: a planted-tank light with a heat sink.
Neighbouring blocks join on **all four sides** into one fixture of any size, so it can cover a whole aquarium.

* It gives light 15 **plus 5** on the part of the aquarium it covers (light 20 under full cover): the deepest red for
  red plants and full growth for the strong-light plants, but also the most algae without CO2 and fertiliser.
* Dye it like the light bar; redstone switches the whole fixture off; dimmer and daylight timer in the tank menu.
