# Fry food

Freshly hatched fry are too small for pellets or larvae. They need living micro food, just like in real betta breeding.

| Fry stage | Eats |
|---|---|
| first half | infusoria only |
| second half | infusoria or artemia nauplii |
| from juvenile on | pellets and mosquito larvae |

One bottle of fry food feeds **20 fry**. Feeding by hand gives you the empty bottle back; the feeder uses it up.
Prepare the cultures in time: they need a day.

## Infusoria

Infusoria are tiny single-celled animals that grow in water with plant matter.

1. Mix a <ItemLink id="bettacraft:swamp_water_bottle" /> with **seagrass or any leaves**: an
   <ItemLink id="bettacraft:infusoria_culture" />.
2. Wait **one in-game day**. The culture ripens in your inventory, in a chest or on the ground.
3. It turns into a <ItemLink id="bettacraft:infusoria" />.

## Artemia nauplii

Artemia are brine shrimp; their dried eggs (cysts) hatch in salt water.

1. **Cysts:** mining sand (also red sand) sometimes drops <ItemLink id="bettacraft:artemia_cysts" /> (2 %). One jar
   lasts for 16 hatcheries. Jars stack (16 per stack, the server can allow 32 or 64); only jars with the same uses left
   stack together, a used jar comes back from the crafting grid on its own.
2. **Salt water:** use a glass bottle on water in an ocean: a <ItemLink id="bettacraft:salt_water_bottle" />.
3. Mix both into an <ItemLink id="bettacraft:artemia_culture" /> and wait **one in-game day in the light** (light level
   9 or more when it is ready).
4. It turns into a <ItemLink id="bettacraft:artemia_nauplii" />.
