# Cranwell's horned frog

Cranwell's horned frog (*Ceratophrys cranwelli*, about 12 cm) of the Gran Chaco is almost all head and mouth: a round,
green or brown frog with small horns over the eyes. It is an **ambush hunter**: it digs itself into the moist ground until
only the eyes look out and swallows whatever comes past.

## Care

| | |
|---|---|
| Tank | a moist [terrarium](../terrarium.md) (a water dish is fine: 90 % land or more) with soil or sand to dig in |
| Air | 23 to 30 °C; **humidity 60 to 95 %** |
| Food | [crickets](feeder_insects.md) |
| Stocking | 2 bettas |

* Most of the time it **sits buried** in the ground of the interior; now and then it hops a few lazy jumps and buries
  itself again elsewhere.
* Two varieties occur wild: green and brown.

## Breeding

None in the tank: in captivity the horned frog only spawns after a simulated rainy season and with hormones. Every horned
frog comes from the wild.
