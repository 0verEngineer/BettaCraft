# Marbled crayfish

The marbled crayfish *Procambarus virginalis* is a big crayfish (about 9 cm) with a brown-olive, darkly marbled shell.
There are **only females**: every adult carries eggs without a male, and the young are **clones** of their mother
(parthenogenesis). One animal is enough for a whole colony, and it grows fast.

## Profile

| | |
|---|---|
| Group | Crayfish |
| Size in the game | about 8.5 cm (0.72 blocks) |
| Temperature | 12–30 °C |
| Water | fresh water, normal |
| Water chemistry | pH 6.0–8.4, TDS 0–700 ppm |
| Breeding | females only: every adult carries eggs without a male, the young are her clones, 3–6 young |
| Growing up | about 5 days |
| Climbs the glass | no |
| Stocking | counts as 0.75 fish |

## In the tank

* It behaves like the [Florida crayfish](florida_crayfish.md): it catches small animals and young fish, pinches slow
  fish near the floor, eats plants back and digs young plants out of the substrate (see there; the same config values).
* **It multiplies on its own:** every adult female carries eggs again and again, 3 to 6 young each time, grown up in
  5 days. A tank fills up quickly: take animals out with the [aquarium net](../equipment/tank_net.md) or the
  **Small animals** page of the tank menu, or keep it with fish that eat the young.
* Very hardy: 12 to 30 °C, it takes almost any water.
* It has no colour lines (every animal is a clone); the marbling differs a little from animal to animal.

## Obtaining

* **[Aquarium net](../equipment/tank_net.md)** in rivers: 0.46 % per swing (about 1 in 220 swings) (config `features.invertebrates.bigCrayfish`).
* **Wild** in rivers, in groups of 2–5 (spawn weight 1) (config `features.invertebrates.bigCrayfish`).
* **Wandering trader** (usual offers): 1 for 4 emeralds (config `features.invertebrates.bigCrayfish`).
* **Breeding** in the aquarium (one animal is enough).

The biomes of biome mods count too ([biome tags](../settings.md)).
