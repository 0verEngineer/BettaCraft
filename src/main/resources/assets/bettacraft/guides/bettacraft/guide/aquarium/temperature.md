# Temperature

Bettas are tropical fish.

| Range | Effect |
|---|---|
| 24 to 30 °C | tolerable: the fish stay healthy and grow |
| 26 to 28 °C | ideal, and needed for breeding |
| below 24 or above 30 °C | stress: after 7 days the fish get sick, eggs stop developing, young stop growing |

## Surroundings

Without a heater the water takes the temperature of the biome, about **25 °C in a swamp**, 12 °C in a taiga, 32 °C
in a desert and 5 °C in snow, plus up to 2 °C warmer at noon and 2 °C colder at midnight. The water follows changes
slowly (within a few hours of play time).

## Heating

[Heaters](../equipment/heaters.md) push the water to the **target temperature** of the aquarium. Set it in the tank
menu between 20 and 32 °C (default 27 °C).

* Each heater warms a number of water blocks (16, 32 or 64); several heaters add up.
* If the heaters are too small for the aquarium, the water only gets part of the way to the target ("only 16 blocks
  for 36 water blocks").
* Heaters only heat. In a hot desert the water stays at the temperature of the surroundings; coolers do not exist yet.

The overview tab shows the water and surrounding temperature, the target and whether the heaters are big enough.
