# Loaches

Bottom fish from South-East Asia: the eel-like kuhli loach is active at night and digs in the substrate, the big clown loach lives in groups and eats snails.

## Species

| Species | °C | Shoal | Betta tank | Breeding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <ItemImage id="bettacraft:kuhli_loach_bucket" /> [Kuhli Loach](kuhli_loach.md) | 24–30 | 5 | yes | Scatters its eggs (fine plants, spawning mop) |
| <ItemImage id="bettacraft:clown_loach_bucket" /> [Clown Loach](clown_loach.md) | 25–30 | 5 | too big | Does not breed in a tank |
