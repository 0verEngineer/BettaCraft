## Where heaters work

* **In the interior:** place the heater in the [interior](../interior.md) of the aquarium. It becomes a heater rod
  (6, 9 or 12 blocks high at scale 16) and shows up in the aquarium as a thin rod. It only heats while its lowest
  block is **under the water surface**; the interior tab shows heaters that are dry.
* **In an external filter:** an [external filter](filters.md) has two heater slots for heaters of any size, for example
  two large ones. They count fully while the filter is attached to the aquarium.

Several heaters add up. The tank menu says whether they are enough ("128 blocks for 36 water blocks") and sets the
target temperature (20 to 32 °C, default 27 °C). See [Temperature](../aquarium/temperature.md).

Breaking one block of a heater rod removes the whole rod and gives the heater back.

For hot places there are [coolers](coolers.md), which go into the same places. A server can make heaters and coolers
need [power](power.md).
