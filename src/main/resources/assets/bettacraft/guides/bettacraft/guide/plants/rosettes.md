# Rosettes and bulbs

Rosette plants grow from the **substrate**: a bunch of leaves on stalks. The crypts send out runners, the Amazon sword grows big, the red and the green tiger lotus grow from a bulb and send floating leaves up to the surface, Aponogeton raises a flower spike; the tiny Cryptocoryne parva and Sagittaria spread into a lawn.
