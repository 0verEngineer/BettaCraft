# Musk turtle

The common musk turtle (*Sternotherus odoratus*, the "stinkpot", about 10 cm) is a small turtle of the ponds and slow
rivers of eastern North America. It **walks on the bottom** more than it swims and only now and then climbs onto land or
a root to bask.

## Care

| | |
|---|---|
| Tank | a [paludarium](../terrarium.md): mostly water, 5 to 60 % land (with a way from the water up onto the land) |
| Water | 20 to 28 °C (the tank's heaters) |
| Air | 18 to 32 °C |
| UV, basking spot | not needed (it uses a [basking spot](basking_lamp.md) now and then) |
| Food | pellets, frozen and live food (by hand or by the [feeder](../equipment/feeder.md)) |
| Stocking | counts like 3 bettas |

* It goes into the tank from its bucket of water by right-clicking the tank, and out again through the tank menu with a
  bucket (or a water bucket on the turtle outside a tank).
* In the tank it walks on the bottom and on the land of the interior, climbs out where the land meets the water and goes
  to the basking spot more often when the air is cold. Build the land as a **slope or steps** (a step at most a quarter
  block of the miniature).
* What it lacks shows in the **Climate** tab and in the log. Water too cold or too warm and the wrong kind of tank cost
  health after a day; too much land or too little only stresses it. After 7 days without food it starves.
* It does not breed in the tank yet.
