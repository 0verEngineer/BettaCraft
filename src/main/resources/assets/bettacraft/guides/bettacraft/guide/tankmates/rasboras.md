# Rasboras

Shoal fish from the swamps and peat waters of South-East Asia, the homeland of the bettas: the classic tankmates of a betta tank. The harlequin lays its eggs under broad leaves. The danios belong here too: the hardy, fast **zebra danio** and the tiny **celestial pearl danio**.
