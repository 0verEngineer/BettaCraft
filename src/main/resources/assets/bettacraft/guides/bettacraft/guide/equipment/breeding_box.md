# Breeding box

<ItemImage id="bettacraft:breeding_box" scale="2" />

Right-click an aquarium with it and it hangs inside the glass. Young guppies, endlers and platys born while it hangs in
the tank fall into it and are safe from the adults until they are juveniles. The [tank menu](../aquarium/menu.md) takes
it out again. See [Breeding the tankmates](../tankmates/breeding.md).
