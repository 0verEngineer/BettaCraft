# Cryptocoryne 'Flamingo'

![Cryptocoryne 'Flamingo'](../images/sp_cryptocoryne_flamingo.png)

*Cryptocoryne wendtii* "Flamingo", a rare bred form: whitish pink leaves.

## Profile

| | |
|---|---|
| Growth form | rosette |
| Grows on | substrate (sand, gravel, dirt, the interior floor) |
| Light | medium |
| Full size | after about 14 days |
| Spreads | no |
| Cuttings per trim | 1 |
| Colour | whitish pink, brighter in strong light |

## Care

* In the substrate; brighter and pinker in strong light.
* Rare: from the aquarium net or fishing in swamps, or the wandering trader.

## Obtaining

* **[Aquarium net](../equipment/tank_net.md)** in swamps: 0.3–0.36 % per swing (about 1 in 278–333 swings) (config `features.plantNetCatches`).
* **Fishing** in swamps: 0.08 % per catch (about 1 in 1202 catches), instead of the fish.
* **Wandering trader** (rare offers): 1 for 5 emeralds.
* **Cuttings**: shears on a grown plant give 1 cutting per trim.
