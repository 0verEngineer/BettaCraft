# Pond slider

The pond slider (*Trachemys scripta*) is the turtle of the ponds, swamps and slow rivers of the south and east of North
America. It comes in two varieties of the one species:

* the **red-eared slider** (*T. s. elegans*): a red stripe behind the eye, dark spots on the yellow plastron; mostly in
  rivers;
* the **yellow-bellied slider** (*T. s. scripta*): a big yellow patch behind the eye, a plain yellow plastron; mostly in
  swamps.

Both breed with each other; every hatchling takes the variety of its mother or its father.

**It grows large.** A hatchling is the size of a coin, a grown female 25 to 30 cm. A grown slider needs a tank of at
least **12 blocks** (water and land) and counts like **8 bettas**; in a smaller tank it is stressed ("tank too small for a
grown one"). Plan the tank for the grown animal, not for the hatchling.

## Care

| | |
|---|---|
| Tank | a [paludarium](../terrarium.md): 25 to 75 % land, half water and half land is ideal; 12 blocks or more for a grown one |
| Water | 22 to 28 °C |
| Air | 22 to 32 °C |
| UV, basking spot | needs a [basking lamp](basking_lamp.md) (UV 3 or more; the open sun through an open top gives 3) |
| Food | pellets, frozen and live food; greens (dandelion, pink petals, grass, fern: [turtle greens](turtle_breeding.md#greens)) |
| Stocking | a hatchling 0.8 bettas, growing to 8 |

* It goes into the tank from its bucket of water by right-clicking the tank; a water bucket on a slider outside a tank
  catches it.
* It swims in the open water, climbs out onto the land and lies under the basking lamp for a long time (longer when the
  air is cold), with its head up and its hind legs stretched out.
* Too cold or too warm water, too cold air and the wrong kind of tank cost health after a day; too little land, UV or no
  basking spot only stress it. After 8 days without food it starves.
* It grows up in 8 game days.
* It lays eggs into sand or soil on the land: see [turtle breeding](turtle_breeding.md). Pivotal temperature 29.2 °C.
