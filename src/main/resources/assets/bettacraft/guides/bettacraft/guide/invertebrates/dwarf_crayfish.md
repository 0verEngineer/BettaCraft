# Dwarf crayfish (CPO)

The Mexican dwarf crayfish *Cambarellus patzcuarensis* is about twice the size of a shrimp (4 cm) and has small claws.
It is peaceful: it does not attack fish, but it takes the food that sinks to the floor, cleans up dead animals quickly
and now and then catches a weak, starving small animal.

## Colours

* **Orange** (the well-known "CPO"): a recessive colour, like the shrimp colours.
* **Wild form**: brown-grey.

These two are the colour forms of *C. patzcuarensis* that exist for real; the bluish *Cambarellus* (*C. diminutus*,
*C. shufeldtii*) are other species, not colour forms, and are not in the game. The grade works like with the shrimp.

## Moulting

Every few days a crayfish sheds its shell. The empty shell lies on the floor for about a day (you can take it out with
the net). For half a day after the moult the crayfish is **soft**: more sensitive to bad water, and bettas may go for it.

## Breeding and needs

* The female carries her dark eggs under the tail for 3 days; 3 to 6 young hatch and grow up in 4 days. Bettas hunt
  the young.
* **15 to 26 °C**: cooler than a betta likes it. In a warm betta tank (26-28 °C) crayfish are at their limit.
* Below 40 % water quality they suffer.
* Stocking: 12 crayfish count as one fish (`stocking.crayfishPerFish`).
