# Tortoise (Naturalist)

The big **tortoise** of the Naturalist mod lives in a **dry terrarium** with a basking lamp. It needs only the mod and the
shipped profile (`bettacraft:naturalist_tortoise`).

| | |
|---|---|
| Tank | a [terrarium](../terrarium.md) without water; a big animal of 1 m: at least **12 × 6 blocks, 3 high** and **100 blocks of land** each |
| Air | 22 to 35 °C, humidity 30 to 70 % |
| UV, basking spot | a [basking lamp](basking_lamp.md) (UV 4 or more) |
| Ground | sand or soil (for its eggs); [heat mats](heat_mat.md) warm the nest |
| Food | **greens** (`#bettacraft:turtle_greens`: dandelion, pink petals, short grass, fern, oxeye daisy), every day and a half |

* Catch it with an empty <ItemLink id="bettacraft:terrarium_box" /> and right-click the terrarium with the full box.
* Feed the greens by hand on the tank or put them into a [feeder](../equipment/feeder.md): it takes one when it is hungry.
* In the tank our walker moves it: slowly over the land and to the basking spot.

## Breeding

A fed, healthy pair breeds now and then (the tank must not harm it: the right kind, warm enough). The female looks for a
nest in **sand or soil** on the land, walks there and lays **2 to 4 eggs**. At a nest of 30 °C they hatch in about 4 days
(warmer faster; at 18 °C and below they wait, above 36 °C they die); the young take the shell colour of their mother.
The tortoises of Naturalist have no sexes, so the nest temperature only sets the speed. Their own breeding and egg
laying is suppressed in the tank.
