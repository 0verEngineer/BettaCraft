# Sponge filter

<ItemImage id="bettacraft:sponge_filter" scale="2" />

The <ItemLink id="bettacraft:sponge_filter" /> is an **air-driven** filter for the [interior](../interior.md): a black
foam sponge with a lift tube on top (2x2x5 blocks at scale 16). Rising air pulls the water through the sponge.

* It needs an [air pump](air_pump.md) at the aquarium; without air it does nothing. With air, big bubbles gurgle out of
  its tube.
* It keeps **12 water blocks** clean and brings some oxygen (about 15 water blocks per day).
* It is **gentle**: it never sucks in fry or shrimp. The filter for breeding tanks.
