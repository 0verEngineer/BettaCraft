# Feeder insects

The insect eaters of the terrarium eat live insects, bred at home like the live food of the fish:

* <ItemLink id="bettacraft:crickets" />: for the [horned frog](horned_frog.md), the [leopard gecko](leopard_gecko.md)
  and the [crested gecko](crested_gecko.md). A **cricket culture** (wheat, short grass and paper make three) becomes
  crickets after a day (`food.cultureDays`), in the inventory or in a chest.
* <ItemLink id="bettacraft:fruit_flies" /> (flightless fruit flies): for the [dart frogs](dart_frog.md) and the crested
  gecko. A **fruit fly culture** (a glass bottle with an apple, sweet berries or a melon slice makes two) becomes fruit
  flies after a day.

Right-click the tank with them: the hungriest animal that eats them takes one. A [feeder](../equipment/feeder.md) feeds
them too. The tags `#bettacraft:feeder_insects` and `#bettacraft:small_feeder_insects` let modpacks add their own.
