# Fish bag and buckets

<ItemImage id="bettacraft:fish_bag" scale="2" />

* **Water bucket**: right-click a betta, a tankmate or a frog and you get a bucket with that animal. Shrimp, crayfish
  and snails go into a plain bucket too (<ItemLink id="bettacraft:critter_bucket" />): right-click them in the world
  with a bucket of water, or take them out of a tank with the bucket button.
* **Fish bag**: right-click the animal with an empty fish bag, with 100 mB of water. It is reusable; empty bags stack
  to 16. The [net](tank_net.md) puts its catch into a bag.
* **Several animals of one species** fit into one bucket or bag, by their size: a bucket holds 32 of the smallest
  (shrimp, snails, mosquito rasboras, neons, endlers), 24 of a harlequin rasbora's size, 12 of the corydoras and platys,
  6 plecos or dwarf gouramis, 2 bristlenoses or yellow tangs and one betta, clown loach or frog. A bag holds half of a
  bucket (at least one): 16 shrimp, 12 rasboras. Right-click the next animal of the same species with the bucket or bag
  in your hand, or take more of them out of a tank: they go into the container of their species first. Never two species
  in one container. Bags of older versions that hold more stay valid (config `features.equipment.bigContainers`).
* **Water**: an empty bucket takes 1000 mB of the tank's water along, a bag 100 mB. A **bucket of water** is used as it
  is and the tank keeps its water, but the water must suit the animal: plain water for freshwater animals, a
  [bucket of sea water](../saltwater/water.md) for reef animals.
* The tooltip lists every animal inside with its sex, stage, colour and, once [analysed](magnifier.md), its genes. Use
  the bucket or bag on an [aquarium](../aquarium/inhabitants.md) to put the animals in, or on water to release them
  (Bettacraft asks first). Released shrimp, crayfish and snails crawl off and live in the world.

The fish bag recipe switches to plastic when a mod with plastic sheets is installed (more bags per craft).
