# Fish bag and buckets

<ItemImage id="bettacraft:fish_bag" scale="2" />

* **Water bucket**: right-click a betta, a tankmate or a frog and you get a bucket with that animal.
* **Fish bag**: right-click the animal with an empty fish bag. A bag holds one betta, a frog, a shoal of tankmates of one
  species or a few small animals, with 100 mB of water. It is reusable; empty bags stack to 16. The [net](tank_net.md)
  puts its catch into an empty bag.
* The tooltip shows what is inside (species, sex, colours, genes). Use the bucket or bag on an
  [aquarium](../aquarium/inhabitants.md) to put the animals in, or on water to release them (Bettacraft asks first).

The fish bag recipe switches to plastic when a mod with plastic sheets is installed (more bags per craft).
