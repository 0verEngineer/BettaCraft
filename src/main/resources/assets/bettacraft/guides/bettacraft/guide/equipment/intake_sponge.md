# Intake sponge

<ItemImage id="bettacraft:intake_sponge" scale="2" />

**Motor filters suck in fry.** In an aquarium with young bettas, guppies or other fry, every running filter with a motor
(internal filter, [corner filter](corner_filter.md), external filter, filter in a cabinet) now and then sucks in one of
the fry (logged in the tank log). The more power, the more often.

The <ItemLink id="bettacraft:intake_sponge" /> stops that: **use it on the filter** (on any block of an internal or
corner filter in the interior, on the external filter, or on a cabinet with a filter). It covers the intake; the filter
cleans as before. The internal filter shows it at its foot. It comes back when the filter is broken.

The [sponge filter](sponge_filter.md) never sucks anything in. The tab **Devices** of the tank menu counts the motor
filters without a sponge. A server can switch the rule off (`features.fryIntake`) or change it
(`internalFilters.fryIntakePerDay`).
